## North star (stable — the why)

**This is a fork, not the main line.** The main line is the MPA *suite* — mpa-conform
(compute layer) + mpa-auditor (viewer layer). This unification is a **consolidation detour**:
the framework is at a natural pause after the two-frame promotion (2026-05-22), and merging
the two compressed docs → one unabridged is how we **plant the flag** — get a single coherent
public artifact out there. Then back to conform/auditor. Not the end of the world; it's a
parkable side-quest that produces something shippable.

**Convergence verdict.** A single coherent spine *does* emerge, and **its organising
principle is the asymmetric triality** — the Boolean→MPA deformation, read end-to-end:
topology (signed coupling graph, Harary balance) → asymptote (open-interval boundaries, the
D→∞ Boolean ceiling). The shape is **"this spine + two projection wings,"** not a flat merge
and not two stapled papers. The two docs already lean on each other (cdv1 = the dynamics
traversing v9's topology; v9's Boolean-section "two faces" already names cdv1's Central
commitment). Two bodies stay genuinely projection-specific and become the wings: the discrete
**algebra interior** (v9) and the continuous **dynamical engine** (cdv1). Everything else
hangs off the deformation spine.

**Gate check (read before executing) — the triality IS the spine, and that is the flag.**
- **Do not demote the triality.** MPA *is* the finite-D deformation of the Boolean (D→∞) ring;
  the asymmetric triality (Harary-imbalance, the sign-topological deformation face) is how it
  goes end-to-end, what **tethers** it (deformation of a known structure — the RM/ANF ring),
  **bootstraps** it, and makes it **falsifiable** (the Central commitment is the planted
  falsifier-of-record). Demoting it to "one typed object" untethers MPA and removes its teeth
  — *that* is the unsafe move. The unified spine is organised around this deformation.
- **Still ungated — because this is already framework content.** cdv1 §Central commitment was
  made framework content deliberately ("so cdv1 is falsifiable here"); v9 §Boolean section
  already names the two deformation faces. We assert exactly what they already assert —
  ignition-necessity, planted-falsifiable — and change no claim → **no §846 dependency**.
- **The deformation has two INDEPENDENT faces — this is what preserves the non-negotiable.**
  *Amplitude face:* chit/D → c/s/r migration → α_s, P_s. *Sign-topological face:* asymmetric
  triality / Harary-imbalance → k_frust → affinity 𝒜. Both are faces of one deformation but
  independent axes; organising the spine around the deformation does **not** couple α_s/P_s to
  k_frust. The standing non-negotiable (α_s/P_s uncoupled) holds automatically.
- **What IS still gated (the real line, narrowed correctly):** the §846 k_frust-PRIMITIVE
  elevation only — claiming k_frust as a numbered empirical primitive that *generates* the
  c/s/r backbone, couples α_s/P_s to k_frust, or is a confirmed substrate-class universality.
  Do NOT assert that in the merge. The Central commitment's own scoping ("ignition-necessity,
  *not* generativity, *not* determinism") is the exact line — preserve it verbatim
  (FALSIFICATION.md §TWO-FRAME + cdv1 §Open conjectures hold the gate).
- **Interface invariance.** The unified claim-set must be semantically identical to the union
  of the two originals. Merge = union of claims, never lossy. A claim change "while we're in
  here" is a separate decision, not part of unification.
