   The vertex regimes are the shadow that moves with the camera; k_frust is what's

2. **Causal priority / bootstrap.** Every other MPA object presupposes a *running*
   holding (c/s/r are operating points of a kernel; the tower is recursion of one).
   Only a *topologically-forced* circulation can persist without a prior maintainer
   (its affinity is drive-independent). So k_frust is the one object that can be
   *causally first* — the seed, not one object among several.
3. **Topologically-protected bit → heredity.** Under the Wall round-trip (R2), strong
   chaos destroys amplitude but only *flips* chirality — a noise-immune discrete
   degree of freedom that survives catastrophe. Heredity needs a carrier that survives
   the collapse of the structure around it.
4. **Singularity of every register.** k_frust is where the Lyapunov fails, the spectrum
   goes complex, detailed balance breaks, the homotopy obstruction lives — the
   exceptional point in field after field. A thing that is the singularity of every
   adopted register is a candidate generator the registers are shadows of.
5. **Structural, not contingent, irreversibility.** Everywhere else broken detailed
   balance is contingent (stop pumping → relax). At k_frust it's *mandatory* — you
   cannot relax to equilibrium without rewiring. The one place the arrow of time is
   topological. ("Stand against entropy" = irreversibility-by-structure, not free.)
6. **Describe vs explain.** α_s/P_s universality is the framework's *empirical adequacy*
   (it describes substrates). k_frust would be its *explanatory core* (why there are
   self-maintaining substrates at all). These need not compete.

## The stress-test (the leaks — equally genuine)

- **Master equivocation:** the grand claim chains *necessary precondition* → *generative
  principle* → *causal origin* as if one. Necessary ≠ generative ≠ source. Oxygen is
  necessary for fire; not its source. Hold the three apart and most altitude drops.
- **Invariance is a label, not an engine.** k_frust is Casimir-like (conserved index),
  not Hamiltonian-like (generates dynamics). And it's invariant under the *observer
  window* only — change the *wiring* and it's gone (substrate-specific after all).
- **Bootstrap holes:** (a) MPA's exposition order ≠ nature's causal order; k_frust is
  itself an *attractor* of the kernel, and an attractor can't precede the system it's
  an attractor of. (b) It needs an $N\ge3$ obstructive cycle — *an already-wired
  frustrated graph* — so the bootstrap regresses to "where do frustrated triangles come
  from," not closed.
- **Heredity: survival ≠ propagation.** R2 shows chirality survives *in the same loop*;
  heredity needs *copying into a successor*. Nothing templates one loop's chirality onto
  another's. A robust uncopyable bit is a diamond, not a gene. (Resolution Ron offered
  later: the heritable carrier is a *latent ledger* (DNA-like, cheap, copyable —
  v9's deferred extension), and k_frust is the *engine the ledger codes for*. Two
  objects, two jobs — this dissolves the leak and is worth keeping.)
- **"Singularity of every register" may be tautological:** the cascade *defined*
  k_frust as the hard case in each adopted field. To break the circle it must *predict*
  a novel singular phenomenon before being mapped, not re-describe a known one.
- **Structural-fragility cost (the decisive one for the artifact refactor):** α_s/P_s
  universality currently stands *independently*. Make it "what happens after k_frust
  bootstraps a holding," and any future attack on k_frust also threatens results that
  don't need it. Centralizing-as-artifact *manufactures* a fragility the framework
  doesn't have. Keep the load paths uncoupled.
