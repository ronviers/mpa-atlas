Open: behavior as the gap closes.
* **RG closure** `proven` (scope-bounded). Meta-ledger flow $\equiv$ running-coupling functional RG: both are flows on a space of generators with an IR-attracting fixed point $\mathcal{M}_2$. Three-step: (i) locality factorization — spectral gap gives $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^b)$ cross-window decay; (ii) capacity preserved as heat-tax coarse-grains the two-mode kernel into its own form at level $n{+}1$; (iii) conjugating isometry $\phi=\Pi_{\text{slow}}$. ## FALSIFIER FORMALIZATIONS

* **§4 surface-code $s\to r$**: distance-3 syndrome traces $s$-aging diagonal sub-threshold, migrates to unit slope across threshold. Falsify: unit slope sub-threshold (no aging), OR shape unchanged across threshold (no migration).
* **§18 avalanche $\tau\approx3/2$**: feedback-coupled NESS with separable timescales self-tunes to SOC, $P(s)\sim s^{-\tau}$, $\tau\approx3/2$; substrates lacking timescale separation resist. Falsify: timescale-separated substrate with robust stable $\tau\ne3/2$, or none at all.
* **§18 meta-ledger branching**: branching ratio $=1$ exactly at $\varepsilon=1$. Falsify: $\ne1$ at the $\varepsilon\ge1$ Wall in any hierarchical NESS substrate.
* **§14 strange attractor**: substrates crossing $\varepsilon\ge1$ show chaotic meta-ledger dynamics (positive max Lyapunov, 3-torus breakdown). Falsify: $\varepsilon\ge1$ crossing yielding strictly stable periodic/quasi-periodic torus (non-positive Lyapunov).
* **§21 MIPS**: high-Péclet ($v_0\tau_R/a\gg1$) holdings cluster at $\gamma_{AB}\ge0$ with swim-pressure + giant density fluctuations, $r$-independent. Falsify: high-Pe clustering without the swim-pressure signature, or high-Pe substrate failing to cluster.
* **§15 chimera**: heterogeneous-coupling multi-mode kernels (esp. non-local lags) instance coexisting sync-$c$ + incoherent-$r$ sub-populations at fixed parameters. Falsify: only uniform sync ($r\to1$) or uniform incoherence ($r\approx0$) accessible.
* **§22 heavy-traffic transport**: the queue-scaling exponent $g(\beta)$ and the FDR aging exponent $\alpha_s=\beta$ are two readings of one $\beta$ — coincident only at $\beta=1$ (both $\to1$, $\langle Q\rangle\sim1/(1-\rho)$), distinct off-Markovian ($g=\beta/(2-\beta)$ or $1/\beta$). Falsify: a substrate's measured queue and FDR exponents fail to collapse onto a common $\beta$ through $g$ (e.g. Markovian queue scaling with non-Markovian aging, or the reverse).
* **§16 tightness universality**: $T=\langle\sigma\rangle\mathrm{Var}(J)/(2k_B\langle J\rangle^2)$ is a class fingerprint (biological active matter $T\approx1$; engineered queues $T\gg1$). Falsify: nominally same-class substrates with arbitrary $T$.
* **§17/§20 $I_{\text{pred}}$ scaling**: $I_{\text{pred}}(\text{chit},Q,\text{model richness})$ scaling differs by class. Falsify: a single functional form fits all classes with no class-specific deviation.
* **§5/§22 hard-vs-soft walls**: soft substrates cross over smoothly (Erlang-B tails); hard-wall substrates snap. Falsify: behavioral substrate with sharp Hopfield-snapping instead of polynomial tails, OR QEC-class with soft blocking instead of abrupt threshold.
* **§19 Turing**: spatially extended $c$-clusters with long-range inhibition yield characteristic Turing wavelengths. Falsify: such substrates failing to pattern, or wavelengths uncorrelated with the activator-inhibitor length-scale ratio.
* **§20 habit-extinction quench**: a robust habit under strict reward withdrawal ($\lambda=0$). Falsify: spontaneous sustained monotonic growth of $V$ without re-exposure, context shift, or cross-activation.
* **§20 auto-tuning form**: a stable stationary holding on a non-PSD frustration-free topology whose measured $\gamma_{s,i}$ give $W=\mathrm{diag}(\gamma_{\text{ref}}/\gamma_{s,i})$ leaving $W\gamma+\gamma^TW$ indefinite/negative-definite.
