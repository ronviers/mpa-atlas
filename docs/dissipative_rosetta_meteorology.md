## Why meteorology is the right worked example

out, a permanent NESS. Its phenomena were *named* by meteorologists with no reference to MPA — so the fact
that they fall on the character primitives one-to-one is a check of the primitives' substrate-generality, not
a fit. And it **illustrates this session's legal close at planetary scale** (see the hurricane row).

## What the table foregrounds

1. **One degeneracy, read from many sides** (the transport-method's load-bearing claim): convection-onset,
   hurricane-genesis, precipitation-triggering, regime-transition are all the *same* ignition/threshold
   structure (c/s/r), seen in different atmospheric guises. The apparatus says they are one object.

2. **The legal close, illustrated at scale.** The hurricane row *is* this session's result: chirality (the
   rotation, Im λ) is the substrate's own intrinsic face — it mints; the *gain* (the energy sustaining the
   rotation) is extracted from the temperature gradient — supplied, not minted. A hurricane over cold water
   dies. "Generative-of-circulation, parasitic-on-drive" is the textbook hurricane.

3. **Rain is the 2B species, found in the wild.** The reservoir/release register the cascade thread built
   synthetically (charge → threshold → release, non-oscillatory, autonomous-ish) is precipitation. The
   atmospheric analogy that motivated widening "autonomy ⊋ limit cycle" turns out to *be* a row in the table —
   the widening was reading the apparatus back onto its own motivating example.

## Honest scope (what this is NOT)

- **Not a character vindication.** Each row is a *structural* re-reading of an established result; no row is
  a measured character round-trip on real atmospheric data. (Same grade as the Banach reference: imported
  math, structural reach, not character flow — `synthetic-is-imported-math`.)
- **Not new physics.** Every dissipative home is the meteorology/physics field's own (Prigogine, Emanuel,
  Charney, Lorenz, Paltridge). MPA contributes the *common coordinate system*, nothing else.
- **Tightness varies by row.** Tight: hurricane-as-heat-engine (Emanuel is explicit), convection-onset

  literal). Looser/analogical: weather-regimes-as-plateaus, the MEP conjecture (itself contested in the
  field). Rows should carry their own confidence if this is developed.
