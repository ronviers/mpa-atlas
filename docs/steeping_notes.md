## Steeping notes (2026-05)

During the Compression Axiom re-grounding session (path-forward edits
landing mpav1 §Compression Axiom on the CHARACTER reading's heat-tax tower + MZ + slow-
manifold construction), several candidate framework moves surfaced that
have not yet stabilized into compressed content. They are recorded here
so they can be picked up if the steep period resolves them into stable
form, and discarded as honest false starts if it doesn't. Status:
exploratory, no load-bearing commitment.

- **Flow-resident number type.** Candidate mathematical type for the
  framework's natural numbers (chit, ε, β_mem, α_s, orbit-affinity)
  whose identity is a non-terminating trajectory under the meta-ledger
  flow rather than a static value at a point. mpav1's value-readings would
  be static-projection faces of these flow-residents. If the type
  stabilizes, asymptotic closure (mpav1 receipts §Asymptotic closure)
  becomes its carrier rather than an independent structural invariant.
  Open: type definition, composition algebra, natural metric (candidate:
  trail-class ρ).

- **Wall as type-boundary.** Conjecture that the Complexity Wall is
  where the flow-resident type loses closure and the framework reverts
  to classical strange-attractor / value-set dynamics. Pre-Wall
  signatures (Cobham-Haken bridge, seven-register chain, asymptotic
  closure) would all be statements about pre-Wall flow-resident
  dynamics; post-Wall, the NRT-chaos prediction (mpav1 §Meta-ledger
  chaos at Complexity Wall) describes the classical regime that fills
  in. Empirical test asymmetric — pre-Wall content is constantly
  testable, post-Wall content harder to instantiate.

- **Two-reframe parallel.** mpav1's "Boolean = D → ∞ limit of MPA"
  (propositional / static reframe) and the conjectured "classical
  strange-attractor dynamics = ε → 1⁻ limit of MPA" (dynamical
  reframe) share architectural shape: richer regime with structural
  content the classical framework cannot express, classical recovered
  at boundary limit, new content non-perturbative. Worth watching
  whether a third axis exists (statistical mechanics? information
  theory? causal structure?).

- **Operator-discovery via regime projection.** The framework's
  $\{C, S, K, R\}$ operators on $\{c, s, r\}$ regimes are arguably the
  operator algebra classical complexity theory has been working
  without — finite-D / finite-ε analogs of class-building operators
  with closed algebra at the limit. Candidate dynamics-side analog:
  $\{C_\beta, S_\beta, K_\beta, R_\beta\}$ on memory regimes
  $\beta_{\text{mem}} \in (0, 1]$. Other candidate regime structures:
  damping (under/critical/over), phase-locking (locked/drift/chimera),
  meta-ledger convergence (convergent/critical/divergent). Memory
  regimes strongest candidate because of seven-register universality
  and the genuine gap in fractional-calculus operator theory.

- **Dimensionless substrate.** With C re-grounded on the CHARACTER reading's heat-tax +
  MZ + slow-manifold construction, the Banach substrate's canonical
  state space is naturally dimensionless (chit, γ_AB/L, β_mem, Q in
  universal-two-mode-kernel coordinates). Two design choices remain:
  α_{σ,0} convention (substrate-conditional vs. framework's
  conductivity unit) and β_mem choice (Markovian primary vs.
  fractional family). Likely resolution after steep period: β_mem = 1
  primary, α_{σ,0} = framework unit, fractional Banach family at
  β_mem < 1 as reference for non-Markovian classes.

These are intuitions in a partially-developed register. They may
crystallize into framework content with proper receipts, or they may
not survive scrutiny. Recording them here preserves the trail without
committing the framework's load-bearing register to them.

## Steeping notes (2026-05)

landing mpav1 §Compression Axiom on the CHARACTER reading's heat-tax tower + MZ + slow-
manifold construction; §Heat-tax tower → Meta-ledger flow in continuous

be picked up if the steep period resolves them into stable form, and
discarded as honest false starts if it doesn't. Status: exploratory, no
load-bearing commitment. Companion notes in mpav1_compressed.md §Steeping
notes carry the static-side content; this file carries the dynamics-side.

- **Flow-resident number type.** Candidate mathematical type for the
  framework's natural numbers — instances scattered across the CHARACTER reading
  include chit-as-orbit-affinity ($\oint v(\theta)/D(\theta)\,d\theta$,
  §The chit unit), the slow-manifold trace ($P_s, \alpha_s$, §Stability),
  Green-Kubo $\tau_R$ (formally divergent at $\beta_{\text{mem}} < 1$,
  §Active maintenance), and the running-coupling β-functions on the
  space of generators (§Heat-tax tower — Meta-ledger flow in continuous
  register). Shared shape: a number whose identity is a non-terminating
  flow on a continuously-renewing substrate, with frozen-frame readings
  as projections. mpav1's value-readings would be static projections of
  these. If the type stabilizes, the seven-register chain pattern
  ($\beta_{\text{mem}} = \alpha_s = $ heavy-traffic exponent, §Universal
  two-mode kernel + §Cobham priority-queue) becomes the universality
  signature: one flow-resident in multiple projection registers. Open:
  type definition, composition algebra under the meta-ledger running-
  coupling flow, natural metric (candidate: trail-class $\rho$).

- **Wall as type-boundary.** Conjecture that the Complexity Wall
  ($\epsilon \to 1$) is where the flow-resident type loses closure and
  the framework reverts to classical strange-attractor / value-set
  dynamics. Pre-Wall closures (§Cobham-Haken bridge, §Asymptotic
  closure, §Universal two-mode kernel's seven-register chain, slow-
  manifold-as-medium reading per §Heat-tax tower → Meta-ledger flow
  in continuous register) would all be statements about pre-Wall flow-
  resident dynamics; post-Wall, the NRT-chaos prediction (§Meta-ledger
  chaos at Complexity Wall) describes the classical regime that fills
  in. Slow manifold reads as the framework's answer to "what strange
  attractors would be if the dynamical-systems theory had been built
  for flow-residents instead of values" — contractively-simple
  destination of constitutively-unfinished flows, rather than
  geometrically-complex destination of Lyapunov-simple flows.
  Empirical test asymmetric — pre-Wall content is constantly testable,
  post-Wall content harder to instantiate (real substrates typically
  die in $r$-regime before the bifurcation sequence completes).

- **Fractional operator algebra on memory regimes.** Candidate
  dynamics-side analog of mpav1's $\{C, S, K, R\}$: operators
  $\{C_\beta, S_\beta, K_\beta, R_\beta\}$ on memory regimes
  $\beta_{\text{mem}} \in (0, 1]$. $C_\beta$ merges two heavy-tailed
  systems into a single dominant exponent (substrate-conditional —
  not generically $\min(\beta_1, \beta_2)$); $S_\beta$ holds two
  exponents separately without slaving; $K_\beta$ generates the
  difference-trail tracking distinct memory structures; $R_\beta$
  severs memory-coupled mode with Landauer-bounded cost. At
  $\beta_{\text{mem}} = 1$ recovers Markovian $\{C, S, K, R\}$ as the
  limit case. Motivation: classical fractional calculus is well-
  developed for single-system Caputo derivatives, but the algebra of
  *coupled* fractional systems is genuinely open. Seven-register
  universality would carry: operators on $\beta_{\text{mem}}$ regimes
  are simultaneously operators on FDR-aging, heavy-traffic, swim-
  pressure regimes. Open: existence of closed algebra at finite
  $\beta$; deformation calculus (analog of mpav1 Theorems 6, 7, 9);
  boundary rules at $\beta \to 1^-$.

- **Secondary regime structures as operator-algebra candidates.**
  Damping trichotomy (under/critical/over, §Stability) with
  composition rules for closed-loop damping of coupled controllers
  — classical control theory has transfer-function composition but
  not a clean operator algebra on damping regimes. Phase-locking
  trichotomy (locked/drift/chimera, §Phase-locking) with chimera as
  metastable mid-state, suggesting a $K$-style distinguishing
  operator on phase-difference trails. Meta-ledger tower regimes
  (convergent/critical/divergent in $\epsilon$) with operators for
  coupling two towers' entropy production. Each potentially yields
  distinct operator algebras at different framework layers; memory
  regimes remain the cleanest first instance.

- **Dimensionless substrate construction.** With $\mathcal{C}$
  re-grounded on the heat-tax + MZ + slow-manifold construction, the
  Banach substrate's canonical state space is naturally dimensionless
  in universal-two-mode-kernel coordinates (chit $= \ln(G_0/L)$,
  $\gamma_{AB}/L$, $\beta_{\text{mem}}$, $Q$). The level-coordinate
  $\nu$ inherits dimensionless-ness from the running-coupling β-
  function integration. Two design choices remain: $\alpha_{\sigma,0}$
  convention (substrate-conditional vs. framework's conductivity unit)
  and $\beta_{\text{mem}}$ choice (Markovian primary vs. fractional
  family). Likely resolution after steep period: $\beta_{\text{mem}}
  = 1$ primary, $\alpha_{\sigma,0}$ as framework's conductivity unit
  (consistent with Banach substrate's identity translation field),
  fractional Banach family at $\beta_{\text{mem}} < 1$ as reference
  for non-Markovian substrate classes.

These are intuitions in a partially-developed register. They may
crystallize into framework content with proper receipts entries
(composition / bespoke), or they may not survive scrutiny. Recording
them here preserves the trail without committing mpav1's load-bearing
register to them.
