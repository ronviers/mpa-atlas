# Play-calling dynamics (NFL offensive and defensive systems) — dissipative Rosetta column

**NESS framing:** drive = rules-enforced time, score differential, and downs (which inject continuous strategic pressure and urgency); sink = clock expiring or turnovers (loss of possession); the steady state = the sustained oscillation of possessions where teams iteratively attempt to accumulate yardage against a responding defense.

**Schools-of-thought preamble** (handle-with-care fields only — §6½ B): NFL play-calling theory is heavily contested between the "Traditionalist" school (which relies on experiential heuristics, momentum, and risk aversion) and the "Analytics" school (which relies on stochastic models, expected points added (EPA), and game theory). They dispute the causal drivers of success—particularly fourth-down aggression and passing rates—but both schools accept the fundamental discrete state-space of the game (down, distance, and field position). Because this is a highly human, strategic system with equilibrium-theory baggage, the core rows below are built entirely on the mathematical formulations (the "adversary anchors") that describe the *regularities* of the game, while specific tactical claims are graded as loose.

**Peanuts** (§6½ A): The core, universally accepted dynamical descriptions are the *absorbing Markov chain model of a football drive* (where down/distance/yard-line are discrete states) (Goldner, 2012), and the *zero-sum run/pass minimax game* (where offenses mix play types in predictable/unpredictable ways to prevent defensive optimization) (Emara et al., 2017).

## What this surfaces

* **Within-field collapses:** The traditional concepts of "sustaining a drive," "moving the chains," and "red zone scoring" collapse entirely onto the **reservoir/release register**; the offensive drive "charges" up the field state by state, reaches a threshold, and "discharges" via points or a turnover (Goldner, 2012).
* **The mint/supplied cut for this field's sustained structures:** The offensive *play variation* (chirality/run-pass mixing) is minted intrinsically by the interaction between the two teams attempting to out-guess each other in a zero-sum environment (Emara et al., 2017). However, the *gain* (the intensity and necessity of the strategy) is entirely supplied by the external drive of the game clock and scoreboard. When a team is up by 30 points or the clock is near zero, the drive is cut, and the structural variation collapses (e.g., exclusively running the ball to bleed the clock).
* **The most striking re-reading:** Play-calling "randomness" or "mixing" is not just statistical noise—it maps to **k_frust**, a structurally forced cycle necessary to break detailed balance so the defense cannot equilibrate to the offense's strategy (Emara et al., 2017).

## Honest scope

* Tight rows (`S, tight`) are explicitly grounded in literal Markov and game theory math utilized across the sport (Goldner, 2012; Emara et al., 2017).
* The formations and fourth-down rows are mathematically loose (`S, loose`) as they either analogize continuous physical mechanics to discrete sports interactions or are subject to intense, unresolved disputes between traditional and analytics factions (Jordan et al., 2009; Lames et al., 2021).
* **What is NOT claimed:** That this translation predicts play outcomes or replaces football analytics. It strictly re-reads the sport's existing mathematical models in the substrate-general coordinates.
